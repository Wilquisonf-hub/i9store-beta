Worked example · demonstration data

A faith channel in South Africa.

A religious broadcaster needs a weekday evening hour for the next six months. Here is what the marketplace does with its answers.

  1. Registers
    Free-to-air in South Africa, DTH satellite to Namibia, Botswana and Zimbabwe.

    Profile: religious / faith. Broadcasts in English and Afrikaans; accepts dubbing and subtitles.

  2. Sees
    17 of 26 titles, faith-safe and family first.

    The 2 real i9 titles, Netmotors and Heróis Eternos, plus 15 demo titles. Hidden: 7 not cleared for the territory or vehicles, 2 rated 18+.

  3. Fills
    60 min × 5 days a week × 26 weeks = 130 slots.

    Second Harvest, 120 × 45 min, one episode per slot: 24 weeks. Bread for the Road, 52 × 30 min, two per slot: the other 2 weeks and more.

  4. Sends
    One request: covers 5 h a week for all 26 weeks.

    Two titles, 6-month licences, free-to-air and DTH, screeners requested. i9 confirms rights and replies with a proposal.
