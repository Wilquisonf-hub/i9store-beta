How it’s calculated. Four steps, no black box.

The same arithmetic runs in the configurator. Worked example: a 5 × 3 m space, front row at 4 m, most people at 10 m.

01 · Pitch and viewing distance

Pitch follows the closest and the typical viewer.

Pixel pitch is the distance between the centres of two pixels, in millimetres. Closer than about 1 m per mm, people see the pixels; from 3 m per mm (about 10 ft per mm) the image looks smooth. These are rules of thumb from i9’s pixel-pitch calculator: a range, not a hard limit.

limit = min(closest, typical ÷ 3) = min(4, 3.33) = 3.33 mm → 2.9 mm

The suggestion is the largest pitch in the list at or below that limit. For a wall that is on camera we keep it under 2.6 mm, and a camera test with a real cabinet comes before the order.

02 · Whole cabinets

A wall is built from whole cabinets.

Cabinets don’t come in fractions, so the calculator fits as many as the space holds and shows what is left. Pick “Closest to 16:9” and it chooses the grid nearest 16:9 instead.

640 × 480 mm cabinets: floor(5,000 ÷ 640) = 7 · floor(3,000 ÷ 480) = 6 → 42 cabinets, 4.48 × 2.88 m

03 · Resolution

Pixels per cabinet × cabinets.

Each cabinet holds its size divided by the pitch, rounded. Until the factory confirms the module resolution, the figure is labelled computed.

i9 COB 1.86: 640 ÷ 1.86 ≈ 344 · 480 ÷ 1.86 ≈ 258 → 7 × 344 = 2,408 · 6 × 258 = 1,548 px

2,408 × 1,548 px is about 3.73 megapixels at an aspect ratio of 1.56:1: larger than 1080p, smaller than 4K UHD.

04 · Processor ports

Ports follow the pixel load.

An LED processor sends the picture to the wall over 1 Gb Ethernet ports, and each port carries a limited number of pixels. NovaStar’s spec-sheet formula gives 659,722 px per port at 8-bit and 60 Hz, and 329,861 px at 10-bit. Brompton’s nominal figure is lower (525,000 px at 8-bit). Copper Ethernet reaches 100 m; past that, fibre.

cabinets per port = floor(pixels per port ÷ pixels per cabinet) · e.g. 659,722 ÷ (344 × 258) → 7 at 8-bit

For your own wall, the configurator turns that into a port count and checks named processors against it (ports, inputs, 4K, frame rate, genlock), with receiving cards, in the processor plan of a free project account.

Questions buyers ask.

Short answers with the numbers worked out. Anything the factory has to confirm says so.

What pixel pitch do I need for my viewing distance?

As a rule of thumb, the minimum viewing distance in metres equals the pitch in millimetres, and a comfortable distance is three times that (about 10 ft per mm). With the front row at 4 m and most people at 10 m, the limit is the smaller of 4 and 10 ÷ 3 = 3.33 mm, so the calculator suggests 2.9 mm, the largest indoor pitch in its list at or below 3.33 mm.

How many LED cabinets do I need?

A wall is built from whole cabinets, so divide the space by the cabinet size and round down. A 5 × 3 m space with 640 × 480 mm cabinets takes 7 columns (5,000 ÷ 640 = 7.8) and 6 rows (3,000 ÷ 480 = 6.25): 42 cabinets and a real wall of 4.48 × 2.88 m.

How do I work out the resolution of an LED wall?

Pixels per cabinet are the cabinet size divided by the pitch. i9’s COB 1.86 on a 640 × 480 mm cabinet gives 344 × 258 px per cabinet (computed; the factory confirms the module resolution). Seven by six cabinets make 2,408 × 1,548 px, about 3.73 megapixels. LED walls rarely land on a standard video size.

Can I feed an LED wall with 1080p or 4K?

If the wall is 1920 × 1080 px or smaller, a 1080p source maps pixel for pixel. Up to 3840 × 2160 px it needs a 4K UHD input or a 1080p source scaled up by the processor. Larger walls are split across several processor outputs or units. A 2,408 × 1,548 px wall sits between the two, so it takes a 4K input or a scaled 1080p source.

How many processor ports does an LED wall need?

Each 1 Gb Ethernet port carries a limited number of pixels. NovaStar’s spec-sheet formula gives 659,722 pixels per port at 8-bit and 60 Hz, and half that at 10-bit. A 344 × 258 px cabinet is 88,752 px, so 7 such cabinets fit on one port at 8-bit. The configurator counts the ports for your wall and names a processor that fits it, with a free project account. Copper Ethernet reaches 100 m; longer runs need fibre.

Which pixel pitches are made for indoor and outdoor walls?

The calculator lists generic indoor pitches from 0.9 to 4.8 mm and outdoor pitches from 2.6 to 10 mm. Outdoor walls need weatherproof cabinets and more brightness. i9’s own COB 1.86 and 1.96 XR panels are indoor models.

How much does an LED wall cost?

LED walls are built to order, so there is no price list. i9 takes your spec (size, pitch, cabinets, processing) to the factory and replies with a price and lead time, for a project in any country. The configurator writes that spec for you.
