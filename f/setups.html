What a setup is, and isn’t.

A starting point for one room, built from live USA listings at their listed USD prices, before sales tax and shipping. It is not a package sales has confirmed for your space. Send us the room and a specialist sizes it, swaps what doesn’t fit and adds the cables and mounts.

The pictures are compositions of each listing’s own product photo, not photos of a real room. We only number gear on a photo when an i9 source names that exact equipment, and none of our project photos does yet.
